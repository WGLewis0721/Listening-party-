Privacy

Short version: this is a demo site. It has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no third-party requests. Nothing you type here leaves your device.

What this site stores in your browser

To keep the demo working it saves a few small items in your browser's own storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to a server. You can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

  • lr:pay:… your demo reservation (an id and a private link code), so your ticket card is still there after a reload.
  • lr:mock:v1 and lr:mock:offset the demo's pretend server: the pretend reservations, and the demo clock if you moved it.
  • lr:picker:v1 your favorite, second choice and note on the concept review page.
  • lr:doorkey and lr:co:… only if the site is ever connected to a real server: the door tool's staff key and a checkout link.

What you type

The demo checkout asks for a name and an email so you can see your ticket card. In the demo these stay in your browser. The "email my pick" and "share" buttons only open your own email app or your phone's share sheet; nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

The camera

The door tool asks to use your camera so it can read a ticket's QR code. The picture is read on your device. It is not recorded, saved or uploaded.

Fonts, scripts and tracking

Fonts, scripts and images are all served from this site. There are no analytics or advertising tools, and no requests to other websites. A Content Security Policy on every page enforces this.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log requests (such as your IP address and browser) under its own privacy statement. The site's author does not receive those logs.

If this ever sold real tickets

This page would be updated first. A real version would store each buyer's name, email and ticket in a database, would leave card details entirely to the payment processor (Square), and would log when the secret address is shown, using a salted hash of the IP address rather than the address itself. None of that is running today.
