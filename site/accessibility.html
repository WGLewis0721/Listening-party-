Accessibility

The aim is for everyone to be able to use this site. It has not been formally audited or certified, so this page says what has been checked and what has not.

What has been checked

  • Text contrast: body text is at least 4.5 to 1 against its background, including text over the animated artwork (measured on the brightest part behind each line).
  • Tap targets: buttons and links are at least 44 pixels tall.
  • Text size: nothing is smaller than 12 pixels.
  • Keyboard: there is a skip link and a sensible tab order, and the main actions (reserving seats, showing the address, picking a concept) work from the keyboard.
  • Motion: if your device is set to reduce motion, the scroll animation is replaced by a plain stacked page and nothing moves.
  • Layout: five looks, at phone, tablet, laptop and desktop widths, without sideways scrolling.
  • Plain language: the guest and door screens use short sentences and no technical words.

What has not been checked

  • Screen readers (VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS) have not been run on the site.
  • Zoom to 200 percent and high-contrast modes have not been tested.
  • The site has been tested in desktop browsers, not on a range of real phones.

Known limits

  • The home page is a scroll-driven animation. Changes between sections are not announced to screen readers; the same content is available as a plain page when reduced motion is on.
  • The door tool relies on a camera. A find-by-name list is the alternative.

Tell me what is broken

If something is hard to use, please open an issue at github.com/WGLewis0721/Listening-party- and say which page and what you were trying to do. The detailed results are in the UX audit.
