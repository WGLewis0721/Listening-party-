Listening Room · brand system

One frame.
A different skin every month.

Every edition ships the same pinned hero, the same night-long scrubber and the same ticket anatomy, so the quality is the same every week. Only the skin changes: motif, display face, five colors, motion feel and voice. Everything below is rendered from the real edition files, so these boards cannot drift from the site.

Frame · never changes

What makes it consistent

  • Hero only. One pinned stage, five beats: Arrive, Sound, Set, Room, Ticket.
  • The night scrubber. A bottom bar with chapter ticks and a clock that runs from doors to close as you scroll.
  • One primary action. The top-bar button yields to the ticket button on the last beat.
  • Ticket anatomy. Plate, head, title, meta, lines, location, QR, perforation, foot, always in that order.
  • Type roles. Display is the skin; body is Manrope; labels are JetBrains Mono at 12px or larger.
  • Data sits on glass. Charts and spec lists get a blurred panel so any motif stays legible.
  • Motion rules. Scroll-scrubbed, intro under 2 seconds, nothing flashes, Reduce Motion becomes a stacked static page.
  • Secret address. Blurred and locked on the hero, decrypted on the card after payment.

Skin · changes per DJ

What makes it theirs

  • Motif. One of five shaders today (spectra, tunnel, sunburst, mercury, spotlight). New ones follow the same uniform API.
  • Display face. Five approved so far, plus a title treatment: case, weight, foil or chrome fill.
  • Palette. Five tokens plus surface and on-accent, each checked for contrast (rules below).
  • Motion feel. An ease curve and an intro stagger: slow and soft, or snappy and hard.
  • Voice. The tagline, the one-sentence statement, and how the set slots are named (film acts, funk hours, anthems).
  • Texture. Grain amount and how strongly the left side is dimmed for text.

Step 1

Pick the sound

Name the DJ and write five set slots with an energy value from 0 to 1. That drives the arc chart and the clock.

Step 2

Pick a motif

Match the room: dots for slow and quiet, tunnel for synths, sun for warmth, metal for polish, spotlight for cinema.

Step 3

Pick five colors

Void, ink, muted, one accent, one signal. The contrast rules below must show green on every row.

Step 4

Check and ship

Screenshot 390 and 1440 wide, check the unlock on the pay page, then deploy. About fifteen minutes once the copy exists.
