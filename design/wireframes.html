The funnel: one scroll, five questions

Each beat answers the next question a visitor has, in the order they ask it. The scrubber at the bottom is the evening itself, so scroll depth and time-of-night are the same thing.

Reading the wireframes

Dashed boxes are content blocks; striped boxes are art (the skin's motif); filled pills are buttons. The only filled button visible in any frame at a time is the primary action: the top-bar button until the last beat, then the ticket button.
