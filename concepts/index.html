Listening Room · Montgomery, AL · concept set

Five skins.
One room.

A monthly listening party has to feel new every time and trustworthy every time. These are the same hero, the same scroll funnel and the same ticket, dressed five different ways for five different DJs. Open one, scroll it, then reserve a seat to watch the address unlock on the customer card.

  1. 1 · Scroll the heroArrive, Sound, Set, Room, Ticket. The bar at the bottom is the evening itself.
  2. 2 · Press ReserveIt opens the pay page, which carries the same skin and builds the customer card live.
  3. 3 · Complete the demoThe location decrypts, the QR appears, and you can save the card or add it to a calendar.

Concept comps. Every DJ and venue shown is fictional, the checkout is a demo, and nothing is for sale. Best on a laptop or phone with WebGL.

The thinking behind them

  • Brand boardsThe frame vs the skin, palettes with live contrast, type, motion, locked and confirmed cards.
  • WireframesThe funnel, five desktop and phone beats, pay page, ticket anatomy, with requirement pins.
  • Requirements to UXEvery ask from the community post and your brief, mapped to a UI decision and a UX law.
  • UX auditSeven fundamentals and the laws of UX, measured on all five skins, with what failed and how it was fixed.
  • ResearchThe design references, the techniques, the type choices, and what I could and could not verify.
  • Brand systemHow a promoter produces a new edition in about fifteen minutes without breaking quality.
